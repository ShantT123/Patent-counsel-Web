PATENT COUNSEL WORKSPACE

Use and review

The workspace helps prepare portfolio strategy, invention intake and business filing reviews. Outputs are working drafts for human review. It does not authorize filings, determine inventorship, provide freedom to operate, verify patent status or calculate legal deadlines. Source references preserve what was supplied; they do not verify source truth. Team members must have authority to share materials with this team and the configured API provider. Numeric calculator results depend on supplied inputs and must be interpreted within the original skills’ limitations. There is no external search, automatic emailing, docket integration or filing functionality in this version.
