# Review guidance

## Business criteria

Company policy may assess product relevance, competitor interest, detectability, and other business factors. Each rating needs a rationale and a source. A subjective reviewer judgment can be a source, but label it as a judgment rather than a verified market fact. A number without support does not become objective because it is weighted.

All calculator scales are oriented so higher means more favorable. Invert an adverse scale explicitly in the input policy rather than quietly changing its interpretation. Do not combine technical merit or likely claim breadth with business scores as if the result establishes patentability. Counsel can record a separate reasoned assessment.

Missing ratings remain unknown. Complete scores may be calculated for draft policies, but approved-threshold status is withheld until policy approval is recorded. The software's approval flag records supplied metadata; it does not authenticate the approving person or authorize a filing.

## Portfolio fit

A mapping records that an invention was assigned to a bucket or reported gap; it does not prove the assignment is correct. Verify the supporting source. Review candidates may share a bucket or an exact technical-term tag. This intentionally simple candidate method may overinclude or miss records. Do not interpret its output as semantic search or claim analysis.

Preserve internal IDs as internal IDs, not patent numbers. Family counts refer to unique supplied family labels, not independent inventions. Records lacking a family ID are counted separately as unresolved, not collapsed into one family or invented families. Counts cover the input extract only. Status dates are retained without independently checking official records. Expired or abandoned records can be relevant background but are not enforceable coverage merely because they match tags.

Actual coverage review needs the relevant claims, prosecution/status context, jurisdiction, and technical comparison by counsel. Claim source references in the data only indicate materials to inspect; the calculator does not read or analyze them. Portfolio fit is not freedom to operate.

## Budget decisions

Confirm currency, budget period, recognition dates, cost inclusions, baseline spent, and remaining committed. Spent and remaining committed must be disjoint; all selected plan lines must be incremental to both. The calculator cannot infer whether two records represent the same underlying expense. Review duplicates before calculation.

Show preparation, official fees, prosecution, foreign filings, and maintenance as separate cost items when available. Do not silently include omitted costs. Prices in the example are invented allowances, not current rates or official fees. Obtain actual company estimates for real work.

The calculator compares explicit subsets. It does not optimize a portfolio or automatically omit low-scoring inventions. A lower-cost scenario is not approval to defer, combine, reduce scope, or abandon work. Those actions may have legal consequences requiring counsel review.
